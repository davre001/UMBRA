# Testnet Faucet

import { Callout } from 'nextra/components';

Umbra runs on Flare Coston2. The **Faucet** page helps you get the three
Coston2 testnet assets, plus a fourth card for bridging in real Bitcoin
(signet).

## What you can get

| Asset | Name | Decimals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `C2FLR` | Coston2 Flare — native gas token | 18 |
| `FXRP` | FAssets XRP — real bridge | 6 |
| `USDT0` | Tether USD — testnet | 6 |
| `BTC` | Real Bitcoin, signet | 8 |

The first three are **real Coston2 assets** minted by Flare's own faucet.
FXRP in particular comes from the actual FAssets bridge, not a simulated
mint. `BTC` is real signet Bitcoin, bridged in via a Noir proof — see
[Bitcoin Bridge](/concepts/bitcoin-bridge).

## How the Coston2 cards work

The page deep-links to [Flare's faucet](https://faucet.flare.network/) rather
than minting anything itself — the point of this app is genuine FAssets
integration, not a fake token.

When you open the faucet link from an asset card, Umbra records your current
balance as a baseline and starts watching. It polls every 5 seconds and
refetches the moment you tab back in, so the card flashes **Received** as soon
as tokens land. No manual balance checking.

## How the BTC card works

The BTC card auto-derives a signet deposit address from your connected
wallet the moment you connect — no separate wallet, no manual "Derive"
step. Copy that address, send it real signet BTC from any signet faucet or
wallet, and wait for it to confirm (real Bitcoin block time, roughly ~10
minutes on average but with real variance).

Once confirmed, the card **automatically** builds, signs, and broadcasts
the deposit transaction itself — no click required. From there, proving
and minting are handled entirely by the backend; your `WrappedBTC` balance
just appears once it lands.

<Callout type="warning">
  **Minting can still take a little while — that's normal.** The last step
  before minting — an on-chain Bitcoin "checkpoint" advancing to cover your
  deposit's exact confirming height — now happens automatically (a
  scheduled worker proves and submits each advance, no admin step), but it
  still paces real signet block production, roughly 10 minutes per block.
  If your balance doesn't update within about an hour of your deposit
  transaction confirming, check whether that worker is actually running for
  this deployment before assuming something's wrong — see
  [Bitcoin Bridge](/concepts/bitcoin-bridge) for the full explanation.
</Callout>

## Requirements

- Wallet connected
- Network set to **Flare Coston2**, chain ID `114`, for the three Coston2
  cards (the BTC card's own address derivation and balance work as soon as
  a wallet is connected, independent of which EVM network is selected)

Coston2 balances only load on Coston2; on any other network those three
cards stay inert.

<Callout type="info">
  **USDC is not available.** There's no official USDC on Coston2 yet. The page
  shows it as coming soon rather than faking a mint, and a clearly-labeled
  testnet mock will ship if and when it's needed.
</Callout>
